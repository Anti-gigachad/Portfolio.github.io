YouTube & analytics

My channels are small and still growing, so this isn’t a growth formula. It’s what I’ve learned by reading my own numbers and changing what I make.

  • Hooks decide a lot. I now write and cut the opening first, then build the rest around it.
  • Retention shows where people leave. I use the retention graph to find the slow moments and tighten the edit.
  • Thumbnails and titles are part of the edit. I design them alongside the video, not after.
  • Posting time didn’t matter for me. I tested it and saw no consistent effect on views, so I stopped chasing it.

Want a second pair of eyes on your channel? I can review your analytics and write up what to try next.
