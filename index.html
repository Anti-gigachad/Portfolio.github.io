Working case study

YouTube analytics, without the growth theatre.

My channels are small and still growing, so this is a working method — not a claim of successful channel growth.

01

Objective

Understand which parts of a video earn attention and where the next edit could be clearer or tighter.

02

Metrics examined

Audience retention, opening drop-off, thumbnail and title context, views by upload, and posting-time patterns.

03

Observations

Hooks matter. Retention shows where people leave. Thumbnails and titles belong in the edit. Posting time did not show a consistent effect for me.

04

Potential improvements

Compare the first 30 seconds across uploads, log edit changes, test clearer title-thumbnail pairs, and review real screenshots over time.
